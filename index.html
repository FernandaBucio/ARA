The problem

Better products start with better evidence about people.

Research behind products, care, and workplace decisions has too often treated male data as the default. The result is an evidence base that can miss how women’s biology, behavior, and lived experience shape real-world decisions.

Why women’s data

Women should not have to be an edge case in the evidence.

Closing women's data gaps is not a separate research exercise. It means building women’s biology, behavior, and lived experience into the evidence teams use to make decisions in the first place.
